# Refund Policy

## Return window

Refund requests for eligible merchandise must be received within 30 calendar days
of the recorded delivery date. Requests received after that period are outside the
standard refund window.

## Item condition

Items must be unused and in resalable condition. This condition requirement does
not remove the 30-day return window. Items reported as defective or damaged should
be referred for review under the defective-item process.

## Refund method

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. A different refund
method requires review by a Customer Operations supervisor.

## Exceptions

Only a Customer Operations supervisor may approve an exception to the return
window or refund-method rules. A support ticket by itself does not record that an
exception was approved.
